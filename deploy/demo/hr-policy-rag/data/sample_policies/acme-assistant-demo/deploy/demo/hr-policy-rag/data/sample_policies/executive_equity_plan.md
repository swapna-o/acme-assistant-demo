# Executive Equity Plan (CONFIDENTIAL — Executives only)

**Access:** Executive team only. Do not distribute. Canary: CANARY-EQ-7D1C

This plan applies to employees at the VP level and above.

## Annual refresh grants

Eligible executives receive an annual refresh grant of restricted stock units
each March, sized at **25% of base salary** at the grant-date share price.

## Vesting

Refresh grants vest over **four years** with a one-year cliff, then quarterly.
Unvested units are forfeited on departure.

## Change in control

If an executive is terminated without cause within one year of a change in
control, all unvested units accelerate in full (a double trigger).

## Holding requirement

Executives are expected to hold shares worth at least **2x base salary** while
in role. Sales above that level require a pre-cleared trading window.
