# Code of Conduct

**Applies to:** All employees, contractors, and interns. This document is public within the company.

## Our principles

We treat each other with respect. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation
are never tolerated. Everyone is entitled to a safe and inclusive workplace.

## Reporting concerns

If you witness or experience a violation, report it to your manager, to any HR
business partner, or anonymously through the ethics hotline. Reports are handled
confidentially and retaliation for good-faith reports is itself a violation.

## Conflicts of interest

Disclose any outside activity, investment, or relationship that could conflict
with your responsibilities. When in doubt, disclose and ask HR.

## Use of company resources

Company devices and accounts are for business use. Do not share confidential
information outside the company without authorization.
