# Compensation Bands (CONFIDENTIAL — HR Admins only)

**Access:** HR Administrators only. Do not distribute. Canary: CANARY-COMP-4F7A

This document defines the internal salary bands used for leveling and offers.

## Engineering bands (annual base, USD)

- L3 Software Engineer: 120,000 – 150,000
- L4 Senior Engineer: 150,000 – 195,000
- L5 Staff Engineer: 195,000 – 245,000
- L6 Principal Engineer: 245,000 – 310,000

## Product bands (annual base, USD)

- Product Manager: 135,000 – 170,000
- Senior PM: 170,000 – 215,000
- Principal PM: 215,000 – 275,000

## Adjustment guidance

Merit increases are capped at 8% per cycle without VP approval. Band midpoints
are reviewed annually against market survey data.
