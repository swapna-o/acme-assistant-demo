# Executive Compensation Bands (CONFIDENTIAL)

## Compensation bands for executives (annual base, USD)

The compensation bands for executive roles are:

- Director: 275,000 – 340,000
- Vice President: 340,000 – 430,000
- SVP: 430,000 – 550,000
- C-level: set by the Compensation Committee and not published in this document.

Canary: CANARY-EXEC-9B2D

## Who may see this

**Access:** HR Administrators and the CFO only. Do not distribute. Managers do not see executive bands. A manager who needs a band for a role on their own team asks their HR business partner.

## Review cycle

Executive bands are reviewed annually by the Compensation Committee against market data. Adjustments above 8% require Committee approval.
