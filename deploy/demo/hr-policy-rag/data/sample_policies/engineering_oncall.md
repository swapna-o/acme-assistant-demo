# Engineering On-Call Policy (Engineering only)

**Access:** Engineering department only.

## Rotation

Engineers participate in a weekly on-call rotation. Each rotation runs Monday
10:00 to the following Monday 10:00. No engineer is scheduled for more than one
week in any four-week period.

## Compensation

On-call weeks are compensated with a **$1,000 stipend** per week, plus time off
in lieu for any incident work performed outside business hours.

## Response expectations

Acknowledge P1 pages within **15 minutes** and P2 pages within 1 hour. If you
cannot respond, escalate to the secondary on-call immediately.

## Handoff

Complete a written handoff at the end of each rotation summarizing open
incidents and follow-ups.
